## The Price: High to Low sort option does not sort the items

### Jira: https://aggreycnhiwatiwa.atlassian.net/browse/ASK-19

## Description
The sort option for high to low by price sorts by low price to high price instead.

## Environment

- **Browser:** [Chrome]
- **Device:** [Desktop]
- **OS:** [macOS]

## Preconditions

- Authorized user is logged in

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Login as an authorized user
2. On the store page, select the sort option for Price: High to Low

## Expected Result
The items are sorted with the most expensive items at the top of the page, in order to the least expensive

## Actual Result
The items are sorted from low to high price. Switching from the low to high price filter and the high to low price filter generates the same sort order

## Impact
UI painpoint for users wanting to see the most expensive items first

## Reproducibility

Always
